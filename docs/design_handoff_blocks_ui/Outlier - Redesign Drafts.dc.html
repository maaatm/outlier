Motion — the new pieces
  • Share. The comment well types itself once on mount (28ms a character, stops on first tap); Post drops the block flat and the well fills with a single line of confirmation in place — the row does not grow.
  • Tabs. Pressing a tab pushes it 6px down into the felt and lifts the other one; rows cross-fade in place over 120ms with no reorder animation, because the ranking is not the story on a tab switch.
  • Your row stays pinned while the list above it swaps, so your position is the one thing that never moves.
  • Switch. The knob is a block: it slides 22px in 140ms and keeps its bottom face the whole way, so it reads as a piece sliding in a slot rather than a light turning on.
  • Box opened. The button presses flat, the status line is replaced in place by the rarity block, and the new accessory drops onto the counter from above with one 60ms overshoot. No burst, no shake, no second colour.

Motion — how blocks behave
  • Press. A block travels down by its own bottom-face height (8px → 0) in 70ms and the drop shadow shrinks with it; release springs back over 140ms. Nothing scales, nothing fades — the piece is rigid.
  • Wells never move. Anything recessed is inert: numbers inside them count up, the well itself is fixed. That is the whole affordance rule.
  • Answer → guess. The unchosen button drops flat into the felt and disappears under it; the chosen one slides up to become the question strip while counters below re-sort into your camp's colour.
  • Slider. The thumb is a block that stays upright; dragging it repaints the yellow fill and re-tints the crowd live, 25 counters at a time, no easing on the count.
  • Reveal. Counters sweep into two piles (staggered 8ms per piece, 420ms total), then the score block lands from above with a single 60ms overshoot — one thud, not a bounce sequence.
  • Award. Yellow "Sharp" block slides in from the left edge; the badge block drops on top of it 120ms later. Histogram bars grow from the bottom of the well in sequence, your bucket last.
  • Wardrobe. Arrow blocks press; the counter itself does a 120ms quarter-turn tip as the face swaps, so the accessory reads as physically re-seated.

2 Three that drop the outline-and-drop-shadow vocabulary entirely
