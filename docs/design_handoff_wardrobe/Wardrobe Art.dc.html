9 Accessories remoulded, and twenty-three more pieces for the box

The faces are right, so they are untouched. The accessories were rebuilt as literal blocks when the app moved to Blocks — a bunny ear became a rounded rectangle tipped 10°, a halo became a brick, a propeller became a bar — and at 18px a rectangle is a rectangle, not a rabbit. The catalogue's own rule is the fix: an accessory is read by its outline. Every piece goes back to its own silhouette and is moulded rather than outlined — flat fill, a bottom face 5 units below it in its rarity colour, a hard cast behind it so cream survives on cream. No outlines, nothing rotated but the counter itself.

Twenty-three new pieces follow the same rule: twelve faces and eleven accessories, taking the pool to 20 faces and 20 accessories — 38 rollable. Every new accessory changes the outline in a way no other piece does; every new face is still dark marks on the disc and nothing more.
